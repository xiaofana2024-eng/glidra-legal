Glidra Privacy Policy

Bundle ID: com.glide.fold · Effective: November 18, 2026

Glidra is a local paper-plane flight journal. This policy explains what the app can access on your device and how that information is handled.

Data we store on device

  • Flight records, model names, notes, and optional photos you attach
  • Optional voice notes and speech-to-text drafts you create
  • Local gift-balance and purchase history metadata for consumable in-app purchases

These items remain in on-device storage unless you export, share, or clear them.

Permissions

  • Camera — used only when you choose to take a reference photo
  • Photo Library — used only when you choose an existing photo or save an image
  • Microphone — used only when you record a voice note or start speech-to-text
  • Speech Recognition — used only when you convert speech into editable text
  • Tracking (ATT) — the system prompt may appear once; this build does not use tracking data for ads or profiling
  • Notifications — the system prompt may appear once; this build does not send notification content

Purchases

Gift packs are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment details. Glidra stores only local purchase receipts needed to show your gift balance on this device.

No account backend

Glidra does not require an account and does not operate a private cloud for flight notes in this version.

Contact

For privacy questions about Glidra, contact the developer through the App Store product page support channel.
